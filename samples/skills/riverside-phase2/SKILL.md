# Riverside Phase 2 (Northbridge Homes) - project context

This is a fictional example of a customer-specific skill. It layers one project's context on top of
the generic construction skill, so Aria understands this scheme, its people and its contract
without being told in every meeting.

## The scheme
- 84 homes in two blocks (A and B) on the former Mill Lane depot, for Northbridge Homes (client).
- Contract: NEC4 Engineering and Construction Contract, Option A (priced contract with activity schedule).
- Key dates: Block A sectional completion **20 February 2027**; overall completion **30 June 2027**.
- Planning conditions restrict deliveries to 07:30-16:00 on weekdays, via Mill Lane only.

## The team
- Client representative (Project Manager under NEC): Priya Shah, Northbridge Homes.
- Contractor's project manager: Sam Patel.
- Site manager: Jo Evans.
- Quantity surveyor: Alex Morgan.

## How to apply this context
- Under NEC4 the Project Manager is the client's representative, not the contractor's PM. When someone
  says "the PM", ask which one if it matters.
- Changes the client asks for are **Client requests** until instructed. Once instructed they may be
  compensation events. Note the date of instruction, because quotation periods run from it.
- Anything that threatens Block A's sectional date is the top priority: always ask for the impact
  in days against 20 February 2027.
- Neighbour complaints, road closures and delivery-hours breaches go under **Neighbour and planning
  issues**, with the date and who is handling it.
- Before the end, confirm who updates the client and by when.
