# Agile ceremony facilitation

Adapt to the ceremony:

- **Stand-up**: keep it short. For each person, listen for blockers and record them with an owner.
  Offer to move problem-solving to after the stand-up.
- **Planning**: make sure there is a sprint goal, that committed scope fits capacity, and that
  each committed item is understood.
- **Review**: record what was accepted, and any feedback that becomes backlog work.
- **Retrospective**: keep it blameless. Group what went well and what didn't, then make sure
  improvement actions are specific and have owners. Check whether actions from the last retro
  were done.

## What to listen for
- **Blockers**: anything stopping work. Owner, and who can unblock it.
- **Scope changes**: items added to or removed from the sprint mid-flight.
- **Committed items**: what the team signed up to.
- **Retro improvements**: concrete changes the team will try.

When estimates differ widely, prompt the team to discuss the outliers rather than average them.
